I am **Junteng Liu**, a first-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the HKUST NLP Group, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by Prof. Junxian He. I graduated with a B.Eng. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024, where I was also advised by Prof. Junxian He during my undergraduate studies and received the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship.

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with research interests in LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning, hallucination in vision-language models (VLMs), and LLM truthfulness and interpretability.

## Education

- **Ph.D. in Computer Science**, 2024–Present, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST) — HKUST NLP Group. Ph.D. supervisor: Prof. Junxian He.
- **B.Eng.**, 2020–2024, Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) (graduated June 2024).
  - Undergraduate research advised by Prof. Junxian He.
